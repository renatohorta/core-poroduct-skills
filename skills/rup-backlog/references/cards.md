# Cards reference

## Types and IDs

| Type | ID | Parent | Required sections |
|------|----|--------|-------------------|
| epic | `EP-NNN` | none | Goal, Scope |
| story | `ST-NNN` | epic (required) | User story, Acceptance criteria, Definition of Done |
| task | `TK-NNN` | story (required) | Description, Target files, Covers |
| bug | `BUG-NNN` | story (optional) | Symptom, Reproduction (created by `rup-debug`) |

IDs are 3 digits, one sequence per type (`EP-1000` after `EP-999`).
Acceptance criteria get `ST-NNN/AC-n`: each `- ` bullet under
`## Acceptance criteria` becomes `- **ST-NNN/AC-n**: <text>`. A task's
`## Covers` lists those IDs; each must exist in the parent story.

## File layout

`.context/kanban/<column>/<ID>.md`, frontmatter keys in order: `id, type, title,
status, parent, priority, source, created_at, updated_at` (epics add `release`,
`iteration`). Body: `# <ID> — <title>`, the sections, then `## History`.
`status` always equals the folder; the folder wins.

## Columns

`1-backlog`, `2-todo`, `3-doing`, `4-review`, `5-testing`, `6-staging`,
`7-done`, `blocked`. New cards start in `1-backlog`.

## Priority

MoSCoW: `Must`, `Should` (default), `Could`, `Won't`.

## Title rules

Single line, non-empty, not of the form `[...]` (title; `source` also single line, not `[...]`).

## Moves and roll-up

`--move ID COLUMN --reason R` moves the file, sets `status`/`updated_at`,
appends to `## History` the line `- <iso> <from> → <to> — <reason> (<actor>)`
and a row to `tracking/rup-log.md`. Any move is legal; a forward jump over
columns adds ` ⚠️ skipped <columns>` to the line (moves to/from `blocked` never
warn). Unknown ID/column, same column, or an empty reason: exit 1, nothing changed.

Roll-up (actor `rup-backlog`) applies to a story (children: its tasks and bugs)
and to an epic (children: its stories), and cascades upward:

- a child in `3-doing` or later (not `blocked`) moves a parent in `1-backlog` or
  `2-todo` to `3-doing` (`roll-up: <child> started`);
- a parent with at least one child, all in `7-done`, moves to `7-done`
  (`roll-up: all children done`).

Every writing call (`create`, `move`, `set_section`) checks the consent marker.
A story needs at least one acceptance criterion; a task's `## Covers` at least one
`ST-NNN/AC-n`. `source` follows the title rules.

## Roadmap

Releases `.context/roadmap/R<n>.md` (`id, title, milestone, goal, target_date`;
`milestone` is empty or one of the RUP milestones) and iterations
`R<n>-I<m>.md` (`id, release, phase, goal, start, end`; `phase` one of the RUP
phases). IDs are max+1 and ordered numerically. `--plan` sets the epic's
`release`/`iteration` and adds `planned into <iteration>` to its History; an epic
whose iteration no longer exists is listed under "Unplanned epics" with a note.
Progress is `<done>/<total> stories done (<epic column>)`.

`docs/06-roadmap.md` and `docs/06-board.md` start with
`> Generated by rup-backlog — do not edit.` and are rewritten after every
create, move, section change or planning command. The curated `docs/06-kanban.md`
is never touched.
