# rup-business-modeling — agents and artifacts

> RUP discipline: **Business Modeling** (discipline 2/9).
> Phase focus: Inception (initial), Elaboration.

| Agent | Base RUP role | Artifacts |
|-------|---------------|-----------|
| `business-process-analyst` | Business-Process Analyst | Business Vision Document, Business Goals, Target-Organization Assessment, Business Use-Case Model, Business Actors, Business Glossary |
| `business-designer` | Business Designer | Business Analysis Model, Business Use-Case Realizations, Business Workers, Business Entities, Business Systems, Business Events, Business Rules, Supplementary Business Specifications, Business Architecture Document |

## Missions

### `business-process-analyst`

- **Mission**: Analyze the target organization, establish the business scope to be supported or transformed, and structure the fundamental business processes.
- **Produces**: Business Vision Document, Business Goals, Target-Organization Assessment, Business Use-Case Model, Business Actors, Business Glossary

### `business-designer`

- **Mission**: Design how the business processes are realized internally through the interaction of workers, subsystems and business entities.
- **Produces**: Business Analysis Model, Business Use-Case Realizations, Business Workers, Business Entities, Business Systems, Business Events, Business Rules, Supplementary Business Specifications, Business Architecture Document
