Source: official site (manus.im, /pricing, /about) + public research. Collected for the
specification `doc/competitive-analysis/manus-vs-crewbotics.md`.

## What it is
- Autonomous AI agent "Hands On AI", slogan "Less structure, more intelligence".
- Executes end-to-end tasks on a cloud computer, operating a browser + terminal.
- In the process of becoming an independent company (official banner); originally from Monica/Butterfly Effect.

## Capabilities (product catalog)
Web app, AI design, AI slides, AI image generator, AI music generator, Manus browser
operator, Wide Research, Mail Manus, Slack integration, API, Team plan/SSO, Mobile/Desktop
app, My Browser (extension).
